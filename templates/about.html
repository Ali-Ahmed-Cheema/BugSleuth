ABOUT

BugSleuth: The AI Incident Tribunal

BugSleuth is an AI-powered software incident investigation platform designed to help developers investigate software failures through evidence, critical analysis, and verification.

Unlike traditional AI debugging tools that simply provide an answer, BugSleuth treats every potential root cause as a hypothesis that must be investigated and challenged.

Core philosophy “Don’t just let AI find the bug. Make it prove its case.”
THE PROBLEM

Why this exists

When software incidents occur, developers often have to work through large amounts of source code, logs, deployment data, and change history while evaluating several possible explanations at once.

Some explanations can sound convincing because they are plausible, but that does not mean they are supported by evidence. In high-pressure situations, a misleading conclusion can be costly and slow the real fix.

  • Large, noisy evidence sets
  • Multiple possible root causes
  • Recent code or deploy changes to consider
  • Difficulty separating facts from assumptions
  • AI-generated answers that need verification

BugSleuth is designed to make AI-assisted debugging more transparent, evidence-based, and trustworthy.

HOW IT WORKS

Investigation workflow

INCIDENT ↓ EVIDENCE COLLECTION ↓ INDEPENDENT INVESTIGATION ↓ AI TRIBUNAL ↓ VERDICT ↓ CONFIDENCE LEDGER ↓ VERIFICATION

1. Project Intelligence

BugSleuth automatically analyzes repository structure, technologies, tests, and other available project context.

2. Auditable Evidence

Different investigators review source code, logs, Git history, CI/CD workflows, and deployment context. Each directly cited item receives an E-NNN evidence ID and is labelled as a Fact.

3. The AI Tribunal

Specialized roles examine the evidence from different angles: prosecutor, defense, and judge. Their reasoning cites evidence IDs, shows the causal chain, and records counterarguments.

4. Transparent Verdict

The Judge records supporting evidence, uncertainty, the strongest defense argument, and the specific evidence that would change the verdict.

5. Verified Proof

The bundled demo runs pytest before and after a patch on a temporary copy. Uploaded and GitHub code is not executed by BugSleuth.

Human review remains essential

BugSleuth does not automatically deploy changes to production. It is designed to support investigation, not replace human judgment or operational controls.

VERDICT SYSTEM

Clear, honest conclusions

CONFIRMED

Strong evidence and verification support the conclusion.

MOST_LIKELY

The evidence indicates a leading explanation, but complete proof is not yet available.

MORE_EVIDENCE_NEEDED

The available information is insufficient to identify a reliable leading explanation.

REJECTED

The available evidence contradicts the proposed hypothesis.

Uncertainty is an important feature, not a failure. BugSleuth is intentionally honest about what it knows, what it does not know, and what would change its view.

IBM HACKATHON

BobBuilders team project

BugSleuth was developed as part of the IBM TechXchange 2026 Pre-conference Dev Day Hackathon by the BobBuilders team. The project explores how agentic AI can support software development and operations workflows with greater transparency, evidence discipline, and adversarial review.

  • Specialized investigation roles
  • Parallel and independent analysis
  • Evidence-based reasoning
  • Adversarial review
  • Code investigation and verification planning

This prototype implements a deterministic, evidence-first investigation flow and demonstrates the concepts important to the hackathon. It clearly distinguishes the current prototype capabilities from future IBM Bob integrations or broader agentic features.
