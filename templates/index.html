BUGSLEUTH

      
AI Incident Investigation Platform

      

        BugSleuth is an AI-powered incident investigation platform built to help developers understand software failures through evidence, critical analysis, and verification. Instead of simply generating a probable answer, BugSleuth treats every potential root cause as a hypothesis that must be investigated, challenged, and supported by evidence.
      

      

        Don't just let AI find the bug. Make it prove its case. A BobBuilders project developed for the IBM TechXchange 2026 Pre-conference Dev Day Hackathon.
